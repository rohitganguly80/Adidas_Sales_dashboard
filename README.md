## 📌 Overview
This Power BI dashboard provides comprehensive insights into Adidas’ sales performance, profitability, pricing strategy, and product demand. The project leverages interactive visualizations to help stakeholders understand sales trends, regional performance, and product-wise contributions.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profit performance over time
- Understand regional and state-wise sales distribution
- Evaluate product demand and pricing strategy
- Assess profitability through margin analysis
- Enable data-driven business decisions using KPIs

---

## 🧩 Problem Statement
Raw sales data alone does not provide actionable insights into performance, profitability, and demand patterns. The objective of this project is to analyze Adidas sales data to evaluate overall revenue, profit, sales volume, pricing effectiveness, and margins.

By leveraging key performance indicators (KPIs) and Power BI visualizations, this dashboard identifies trends, performance gaps, and opportunities for strategic improvement.

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

1. **Total Sales**  
   Understand overall sales performance over time.

2. **Total Profit**  
   Evaluate the total profit generated across regions and products.

3. **Units Sold**  
   Analyze sales volume to understand product demand.

4. **Average Price per Unit**  
   Assess pricing strategy effectiveness.

5. **Average Margin**  
   Measure overall profitability of sales.

---

## 📈 Business Requirements & Visualizations

### Charts Implemented

1. **Total Sales by Month**  
   *Objective:* Identify seasonal trends and peak sales periods.  
   *Chart Type:* Area Chart

2. **Total Sales by State**  
   *Objective:* Analyze geographical sales distribution.  
   *Chart Type:* Filled Map

3. **Total Sales by Region**  
   *Objective:* Compare regional contribution to total sales.  
   *Chart Type:* Donut Chart

4. **Total Sales by Product**  
   *Objective:* Evaluate product-wise sales performance.  
   *Chart Type:* Bar Chart

## ▶️ How to Use This Dashboard
1. Download the `.pbix` file from this repository.
2. Open it using **Power BI Desktop**.
3. Use slicers and filters to explore data across time, region, and products.
4. Modify the dataset to refresh insights dynamically.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Certain regions contribute significantly more to total sales, indicating strong regional markets.
- Sales trends show clear seasonal peaks, useful for demand forecasting.
- A small set of products contributes a large portion of total revenue.
- Pricing and margin analysis highlights opportunities to improve profitability.

---

## 💡 Recommendations
- Focus marketing and inventory planning on high-performing regions and products.
- Use seasonal trends to optimize production and supply chain planning.
- Revisit pricing strategy for low-margin products.
- Expand presence in underperforming states with targeted campaigns.
